# Owner's Guide — Vishal Mens Parlour Website

Welcome! This guide shows you how to run the website yourself, without needing a developer.

## 1. Logging in

- Go to **yourdomain.com/admin/login**
- Enter your email and password (given to you at handover)
- Click **Login**
- Forgot your password? Click **Forgot Password?** and check your email for a reset link

## 2. The dashboard

The dashboard shows:

- **Today's Revenue** — money coming in from bookings today
- **This Week** — rolling 7-day revenue
- **Today's Bookings** — how many customers are coming today
- **Pending** — bookings waiting for you to confirm
- **Customers** — total unique customers you've served
- **No-Show Rate** — % of past bookings that were cancelled or not completed
- **Today's Schedule** — list of appointments for today
- **Tomorrow** — appointments for tomorrow

## 3. Managing appointments

Go to **Appointments** in the sidebar.

- **Filter** by status (Pending, Confirmed, etc.) or date range
- **Confirm** a pending booking by clicking the green checkmark
- **Complete** a booking (after the customer leaves) with the checkmark
- **Cancel** any booking with the red X
- **Call** or **WhatsApp** the customer directly from the row
- **New Appointment** — for walk-ins, click the top-right button and fill the form
- Older appointments are paginated — 25 per page

## 4. Managing services

Go to **Services** in the sidebar.

- **Add a new service** — click "+ New Service", fill name, price, duration, description, image
- **Edit** — click any service to change its details
- **Turn on/off** — use the toggle to hide a service from customers (without deleting it)
- **Featured** — mark a service to appear on the homepage

## 5. Managing your gallery and website content

Go to **Website Content** in the sidebar.

- Update salon name, phone, WhatsApp, address, opening hours
- Add gallery photos with the drag-drop uploader
- Manage offers (special discounts shown on homepage)
- Change hero images per section

## 6. Blocking holidays / time off

Go to **Calendar** in the sidebar.

- Click "Block Time"
- Pick start and end date/time
- Add a reason (e.g. "Diwali holiday", "Team training")
- Save — customers won't see these times as available to book

## 7. Customer list

Go to **Customers** in the sidebar.

- See every customer who has booked
- Sorted by most recent visit
- Click a customer to see all their past appointments and total spend

## 8. Handling bookings that come in

Whenever a customer books online:

1. You'll get an **email** at your registered address with the booking details.
2. The customer gets a **confirmation email** with a link to reschedule or cancel.
3. The booking appears in **Appointments** as PENDING.
4. Confirm it → customer stays informed via the manage-booking link.

## 9. Common tasks — quick reference

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| See today's bookings | Dashboard → Today's Schedule |
| Add a new haircut service | Services → + New Service |
| Change my phone number | Website Content → Business Settings |
| Take Sunday off next week | Calendar → Block Time |
| See who is coming tomorrow | Dashboard → Tomorrow |
| Change my password | Login page → Forgot Password |
| Add a photo to the gallery | Website Content → Gallery |
| See how much I earned this week | Dashboard → This Week |

## 10. What to do if something is wrong

If:

- Customers say they can't book → Check **Website Content → Booking Settings → Booking Enabled** is ON
- OTP not arriving → Contact your developer, MSG91 credit may be out
- Emails not arriving → Contact your developer, Resend limit may be hit
- Anything else broken → Take a screenshot and message your developer
